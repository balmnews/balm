Whether a formal ceasefire agreement has been fully abandoned remains unclear from available reporting. The conflict is driving significant economic consequences inside the United States. Energy costs accounted for more than 60 percent of the increase, according to Labor Department figures. Core inflation, which excludes food and energy, rose a more modest 2.9 percent annually. President Trump, asked about the inflation data, said he was not concerned. The economic ripple effects are extending beyond the United States. Antarctic Winter Temperature Record Broken as Region Logs Reading Above 15°C

A temperature above 15 degrees Celsius was recorded at Argentina's Esperanza base on June 6, shattering the previous Antarctic winter heat record and prompting concern among climate scientists about the pace of change in the region. A new winter heat record for Antarctica was set on June 6 when the Argentinian Esperanza base on the Trinity Peninsula recorded a temperature above 15 degrees Celsius, well above the previous winter high for the region. Scientists described the reading as 'very strange,' noting that conditions included snow melt and rainfall on glaciers that would normally be frozen. Antarctica's winter runs from June through August in the Southern Hemisphere. Researchers say the record adds to a pattern of unusual temperature anomalies in the region in recent years, and raises questions about the pace of climate-related changes in one of the world's most climate-sensitive environments. The Antarctic ice sheet holds enough frozen water to raise global sea levels significantly if it were to melt substantially.
