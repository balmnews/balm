The US Department of Agriculture confirmed the first case of the New World screwworm fly in domestic cattle in roughly six decades, discovered in a calf in Texas. The parasite was eradicated from the United States in the 1960s through a sustained federal program, and its reemergence is considered a significant threat to the livestock industry.
