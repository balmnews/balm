The Federal Aviation Administration has granted certification to Boeing's 737 Max 10, the largest variant in the 737 Max family, allowing airlines to begin putting the plane into service. The approval comes nearly ten years after Boeing first unveiled the aircraft and follows a prolonged regulatory process shaped in part by the broader scrutiny of Boeing's safety practices after earlier 737 Max crashes in 2018 and 2019 that killed 346 people.
