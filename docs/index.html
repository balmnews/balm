If the hypotheses themselves are wrong, it would call into question a broader set of cardiovascular research directions that have guided drug development for years. Neither company has indicated whether they intend to pursue further development of the failed compounds. The drugs had attracted significant attention from cardiologists and investors, and their failure will likely prompt reassessment of related research pipelines in the cardiovascular field.
