The European Space Agency's Euclid mission, launched in July 2023 with the goal of creating the largest three-dimensional map of the universe ever assembled, has released its first major dataset. The release covers more than 900 million galaxies and includes approximately 15,000 potential instances of phenomena that researchers say could help unlock understanding of what scientists call the dark universe — the roughly 95 percent of the cosmos that appears to consist of dark matter and dark energy, neither of which has been directly detected.
