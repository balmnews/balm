The outcome represents a significant realignment in a country that sits at the intersection of EU accession aspirations and Russian influence in Eastern Europe. Serbia's political trajectory has direct implications for European stability and the broader question of Balkan integration into Western institutions.

Cancer vaccines work by training the immune system to recognize and attack specific cancer cells. The approach differs from traditional treatments such as chemotherapy and radiation, which broadly target dividing cells. Melanoma research provided early evidence that personalized cancer vaccines, designed to match a patient's specific tumor mutations, could meaningfully improve outcomes. Researchers are now investigating whether similar approaches can be adapted for cancers that are currently far more difficult to treat.
