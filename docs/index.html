The COP31 United Nations climate summit opened Monday in Turkey, bringing together heads of government, ministers, and senior officials from nearly 190 countries for two weeks of negotiations running through November 20. Major disagreements among delegations include questions about financing for developing nations, the pace of fossil fuel phase-down commitments, and the role of carbon markets. Turkey assumed hosting duties after a prolonged dispute with Australia over which country would hold the summit. With the US outside the process, observers note that the absence of the world's second-largest cumulative emitter limits what the talks can formally achieve, though bilateral and multilateral deals struck on the sidelines may still carry weight.

Democratic Midterm Gains in Key States Could Advance National Popular Vote Compact

Democratic electoral wins in several swing states have created new trifectas that could push the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact closer to the 270 electoral vote threshold needed to take effect, potentially changing how the US selects its president by 2028. The Democratic Party's gains in state-level races during this week's midterm elections have produced new trifectas — where Democrats control the governorship and both legislative chambers — in a number of swing states. Those gains could bring the National Popular Vote Interstate Compact closer to activation. The compact is an agreement among states to award their electoral votes to whichever presidential candidate wins the national popular vote, regardless of the state's own outcome. It takes effect only when signatory states collectively hold at least 270 electoral votes, the number needed to win the presidency. If newly Democratic-controlled states choose to join, the compact could reach that threshold before the 2028 presidential election, representing a significant structural change to how Americans select a president. The compact has been debated for years as a way to effectively eliminate the Electoral College without a constitutional amendment, and its potential activation is expected to face legal challenges.
