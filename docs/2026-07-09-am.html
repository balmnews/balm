Michigan's Department of Health and Human Services reported 992 cases as of this week. Researchers note that raspberries' surface microhairs can trap the parasite, and that standard washing is insufficient to eliminate it. The CDC indicated no elevated risk in some other regions, such as the San Francisco Bay Area. Researchers warn that commercial deep-sea mineral extraction, which is being proposed or developed in several regions, could drive more than half of these species to extinction before they are even fully documented by science. The update also flagged newly endangered desert frogs and other species affected by habitat degradation and climate change. Scientists noted an overarching pattern: species have evolved remarkable adaptations to survive in extreme or specialized environments, but those adaptations offer no protection against the pace and scale of direct human habitat destruction.
