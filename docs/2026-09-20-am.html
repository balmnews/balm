Three people have died from malaria in Germany in what health officials are examining as cases of so-called airport malaria — a rare phenomenon in which malaria-carrying mosquitoes are transported aboard aircraft from endemic regions and bite individuals who have not traveled to those areas. The cases are linked to Frankfurt Airport. Airport malaria is a recognized but uncommon occurrence in which mosquitoes survive the flight in luggage compartments or passenger cabins and subsequently transmit the parasite to people in non-endemic countries. The fatalities are notable because malaria is not typically present in Germany, and diagnosis may be delayed when clinicians do not initially consider it as a possibility for patients without travel history. Health authorities have not indicated that the cases represent a broader local outbreak or ongoing transmission risk.
