Federal Reclassification of Medical Marijuana Opens Tax Benefits and New Research Pathways

The Justice Department's decision to downgrade the federal drug classification for medical cannabis allows marijuana businesses to claim certain federal tax deductions and enables new research programs at state universities. The U.S. Department of Justice has downgraded the federal drug classification for medical cannabis, a change with practical consequences for the industry and for research. Medical marijuana businesses will now be able to claim certain federal tax benefits that were previously unavailable due to the drug's prior scheduling. The reclassification also opens the door for new cannabis research at state universities, which were previously restricted by federal law. The change does not legalize recreational cannabis at the federal level and does not affect state-level laws, which vary widely. The decision represents one of the most significant shifts in federal marijuana policy in decades.
