Trump Moves to Cancel $810 Million in Congressionally Approved Spending

President Trump announced plans to cancel $810 million in spending that had been authorized by Congress, a move some members of his own party have described as legally impermissible. President Trump announced plans to rescind $810 million in spending that had been approved by Congress, drawing objections from some Republican lawmakers who called the move illegal. The administration has not detailed which specific programs or agencies would be affected by the cancellations. The action involves what is known as impoundment — the executive branch's refusal to spend money that Congress has appropriated — a practice that was significantly restricted by the Impoundment Control Act of 1974, which Congress passed in response to similar actions by President Nixon. Legal experts have noted that unilaterally canceling congressionally approved spending raises constitutional questions about the separation of powers. The cancellations, if carried out, would affect federally funded programs whose budgets were set through the legislative process. Both Democratic and some Republican lawmakers have signaled they may challenge the move, and legal action is anticipated.
