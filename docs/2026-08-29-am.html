Researchers have reported that an experimental gene-editing treatment administered as a single dose substantially lowered LDL cholesterol — commonly associated with cardiovascular disease risk — in a small group of study participants. The therapy works by editing the genome to reduce the liver's production of a protein linked to elevated LDL levels. Larger trials would be needed to establish safety and efficacy more broadly.
