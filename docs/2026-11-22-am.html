The BepiColombo mission, a joint project of the European Space Agency and the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency, has successfully entered orbit around Mercury, the solar system's innermost planet. It is only the third spacecraft in history to reach Mercury and the first to do so in more than ten years. Mercury is the smallest of the solar system's eight planets and among the least studied, in part because its proximity to the sun makes it difficult to reach with spacecraft. BepiColombo carries scientific instruments designed to study Mercury's surface composition, magnetic field, and interior structure. Data from the mission is expected to improve understanding of how rocky planets close to stars form and evolve.
