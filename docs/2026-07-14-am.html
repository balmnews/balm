President Trump signed an executive order sharply reducing the size of two national monuments in Utah: Bears Ears and Grand Staircase-Escalante. Trump also reduced Bears Ears during his first term, and that action was reversed by President Biden. The move is part of a broader Republican effort to reshape federal land management policy, with critics arguing it undermines protections for culturally and ecologically significant public lands, and supporters arguing it returns land to state and local control and opens economic opportunities.
