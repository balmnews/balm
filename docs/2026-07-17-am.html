The Trump administration has asked the Supreme Court to direct a lower court to reconsider its ruling protecting Temporary Protected Status for Venezuelan and Haitian nationals living in the United States. The Trump administration filed a petition with the Supreme Court asking the justices to order a lower federal court to reconsider its decision protecting Temporary Protected Status, known as TPS, for nationals from Venezuela and Haiti. TPS is a humanitarian program that allows people from designated countries experiencing conflict, natural disasters, or other extraordinary conditions to live and work legally in the United States on a temporary basis. The Biden administration significantly expanded TPS designations for Venezuela and Haiti; the Trump administration has sought to end or curtail those designations. The legal dispute reflects a broader conflict between the executive branch's claimed authority to terminate TPS designations and federal court rulings that have placed limits on how quickly or broadly those terminations can be implemented. Researchers announced the discovery of an atmosphere around an Earth-sized planet 49 light-years away, with conditions that could potentially allow liquid water on its surface. Scientists have confirmed the presence of an atmosphere around LHS 1140b, an Earth-sized exoplanet located approximately 49 light-years from our solar system. The finding is considered a significant step in the search for potentially habitable worlds beyond Earth. Atmospheres have previously been detected around gas giant exoplanets and so-called 'super-Earths,' but finding one around a planet closer in size to Earth is less common. Researchers said the atmospheric conditions of LHS 1140b are consistent with the possibility of liquid water on the planet's surface, though this has not been confirmed. The discovery does not constitute evidence of life, but it narrows the field of candidates worthy of further study with next-generation telescopes. The James Webb Space Telescope is among the instruments capable of analyzing exoplanet atmospheres in more detail.
