The rate is among the highest recorded since the 2008 financial crisis. The yield on 10-year US Treasury bonds — one of the most closely watched interest rates in global finance — touched 5% on Monday, a level not seen since 2023 and recorded only once since the 2008 financial crisis. The move reflects an intensifying sell-off in the global bond market, with investors pulling back from government debt as oil prices rise amid widening conflict in the Middle East. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent had recently signaled confidence that bond markets would stabilize, a position sources describe as having been challenged by the market's movement.
