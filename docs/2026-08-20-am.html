The trial results were described by the companies as a significant development for treating one of the deadliest forms of skin cancer. The vaccine works on the same mRNA technology principle as COVID vaccines but is tailored to each patient's tumor profile, making it a different class of treatment than conventional vaccines. Further trials and regulatory review will be required before the treatment could become widely available.
