The Trump administration has asked the Supreme Court to take up a case that would define when and how federal courts can vacate actions taken by federal agencies, a question with broad implications for regulatory authority. The Trump administration has filed a petition asking the Supreme Court to address the scope of federal courts' authority to vacate — formally nullify — actions taken by federal agencies. The question has significant implications for how courts can respond to challenges against executive branch regulatory decisions. Currently, courts in some cases have vacated agency rules entirely rather than limiting relief to the specific parties in a lawsuit. The administration is seeking to restrict this practice, which it argues gives courts excessive power to unilaterally undo federal policy. A ruling in the administration's favor could make it substantially harder for challengers to block or reverse agency actions across a wide range of regulatory areas, from environmental rules to immigration to financial regulation.
