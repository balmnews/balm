Justice Department Stops Criminal Prosecutions for Illegal Truck Emissions Devices

The Justice Department has stopped bringing criminal prosecutions against people who install defeat devices on diesel trucks — equipment that increases performance but disables pollution controls. The Justice Department has announced it will no longer pursue criminal charges against individuals who install defeat devices on diesel pickup trucks. These devices disable factory-installed emissions controls, making vehicles faster and more fuel-efficient but also significantly increasing the pollutants they release. Civil and regulatory enforcement may still be possible through other agencies, but the change ends the threat of criminal prosecution for what had previously been treated as a federal offense. Environmental regulators and public health advocates have raised concerns about the pollution implications of the policy shift, particularly given the scale of defeat-device use in the United States.
